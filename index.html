Solar solutions

A system that fits the way you use power.

Different properties and energy habits call for different solar configurations. Compare the main options and ask for a site-specific recommendation.

⌁

On-Grid Solar

A grid-connected option for properties that use the electricity network. System design and export arrangements depend on local utility requirements.

▣

Off-Grid Solar

A standalone setup designed around solar generation and battery storage. Sizing depends on your essential loads and the backup time you need.

Discuss off-grid solar ↗

Hybrid Solar

A combined solar and storage approach that may also work with the grid. Backup capability depends on the inverter, battery and selected circuits.
